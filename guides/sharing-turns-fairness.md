# Sharing, turns and fairness

Keywords: share, sharing, turns, taking turns, turn, toys, fair, fairness, unfair, equal, same, need, needs

## Key points
- Sharing often means taking turns, not giving something away for good.
- A child can finish their turn before passing a toy on.
- Special belongings can be put away so not everything has to be shared.
- Fair does not always mean the same; sometimes it means each person gets what they need, like glasses for someone who can't see the board.
- Notice and praise moments when children take turns.

## Avoid
- Forcing a child to hand over a toy immediately.
- Explaining fairness only in abstract terms to young children; use a concrete example.
