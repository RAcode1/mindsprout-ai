# Money and saving

Keywords: money, save, saving, savings, spend, spending, needs, wants, budget, credit, credit card, interest, allowance, birthday money, buy, store

## Key points
- Saving means keeping some money instead of spending all of it right away.
- Savings give you choices later and a cushion for surprises.
- Needs are things we must have, like food and a home; wants are nice to have, and it is okay to want things.
- Families make choices about what to buy; that is normal, not a reason for guilt.
- Credit cards are borrowed money; paying only the minimum costs much more over time because of interest.

## Avoid
- Shaming children for wanting things.
- Implying a family is poor or rich because of its choices.
- Recommending specific financial products.
