# Self-worth and body image

Keywords: self-worth, confidence, body image, appearance, looks, comparing, comparison, social media, likes, followers, being yourself, authentic, fitting in, thin, weight, eating, meals

## Key points
- Social media shows edited highlights, not real life.
- A person's worth does not depend on likes, looks or popularity.
- Being yourself means keeping your values while staying open to helpful feedback.
- Bodies need regular meals to grow, learn and play.
- Talk to a trusted adult if comparing yourself to others starts to hurt.

## Avoid
- Endorsing skipping meals or dieting to look a certain way.
- Lecturing teens instead of showing their experience authentically.
