# Gratitude

Keywords: gratitude, grateful, thankful, thanks, thank you, appreciate, appreciation, notice, good things

## Key points
- Gratitude means noticing the good things and people in your life, even small ones.
- Saying thank you or making a card lets people know you appreciate them.
- A daily habit, like sharing one good thing at dinner, builds gratitude.

## Avoid
- Guilt-tripping ("be grateful because others have less").
