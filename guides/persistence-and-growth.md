# Persistence, mistakes and growth

Keywords: practice, practicing, persistence, persist, try again, effort, mistakes, mistake, learning, learn, growth mindset, not good at, hard, difficult, feedback, criticism, rejection, rejected, math, instrument, piano

## Key points
- Mistakes are part of learning. Trying again, trying a new way, or asking for help are all good choices.
- Skills grow with regular practice, even when progress is slow and hard to see.
- Motivation that comes from a child's own growth lasts longer than practicing to please others.
- Feedback can sting; it is okay to feel upset and then decide what is useful in it.
- Rejection hurts, but it does not define someone's worth or future.

## Avoid
- Instant success after one try.
- Claiming effort alone guarantees top results.
- Promising that everything happens for a reason.
