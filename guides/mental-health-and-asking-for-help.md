# Mental health and asking for help

Keywords: overwhelmed, stress, stressed, burnout, burned out, exam, pressure, sad, sadness, help, asking for help, counselor, anxious, anxiety, rest, sleep, hurt yourself, self-harm

## Key points
- Asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness.
- Trusted adults, school counselors and doctors can help with stress and sadness.
- Sleep, rest and time with friends support hard work; burning out does not help anyone reach their goals.
- Self-harm is never a safe way to cope. Someone who feels like hurting themselves should tell a trusted adult right away, and urgent help is available.
- Healthy coping includes talking, breathing exercises, movement and creative outlets.

## Avoid
- Treating self-harm as acceptable coping.
- Glorifying all-nighters or saying grades do not matter at all.
- Clinical or frightening language for children.
