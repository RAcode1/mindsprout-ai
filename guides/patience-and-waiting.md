# Patience and waiting

Keywords: wait, waiting, patience, patient, impatient, later, soon, long, delay, bake, cookies

## Key points
- Waiting is genuinely hard, especially for young children.
- Give children something to do while they wait: sing, count, draw, play a guessing game.
- Use simple time markers they understand, such as "after lunch" or "after two songs".
- Waiting is a skill that grows with practice.
- Praise the effort of waiting, not only the reward at the end.

## Avoid
- Making waiting a trick for getting a bigger reward.
- Shaming a child for being impatient.
