# Peer pressure

Keywords: peer pressure, dare, dares, fitting in, fit in, say no, popular, everyone else, others doing it, influence, sleepover

## Key points
- It is okay to say no to something that feels wrong, even if others are doing it.
- Real friends respect your choices.
- Having a simple phrase ready, like "no thanks, I'm good", makes saying no easier.
- It helps to have a trusted adult to talk to.
- Leaving a situation that feels unsafe is always allowed.

## Avoid
- Including drugs, alcohol or dangerous dares in stories for young children.
- Showing that saying no costs a child all their friends.
