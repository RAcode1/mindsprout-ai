# Big feelings

Keywords: feelings, emotions, mad, angry, sad, upset, frustrated, jealous, bored, boredom, calm, happy, positive thinking, disappointed

## Key points
- All feelings are okay. Limits apply to actions, not feelings: feeling mad is fine, hitting is not.
- Naming a feeling out loud helps a child calm down and helps adults understand.
- Simple calming tools work at any age: slow breaths, a quiet spot, a hug, talking it through.
- Feelings come and go. A hard feeling does not have to be fixed right away.
- Hope and effort can sit alongside sadness or worry; one does not cancel the other.

## Avoid
- Telling a child not to feel something, such as "don't be sad".
- Pretending everything is fine, or suggesting negative feelings should disappear.
- Shaming a child for crying or being upset.
