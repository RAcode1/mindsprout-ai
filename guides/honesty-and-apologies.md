# Honesty and apologies

Keywords: truth, honest, honesty, lie, lying, mistake, sorry, apologize, apology, broke, broken, accident, accidentally, fix

## Key points
- Telling the truth after a mistake builds trust, even when it feels scary.
- Respond to honesty calmly so children learn it is safe to tell the truth.
- A good apology names what happened and offers to help fix it.
- Making a mistake does not make someone a bad person.
- Adults can model honesty by admitting their own mistakes.

## Avoid
- Harsh punishment that teaches children to hide mistakes.
- Stories where lying works out well.
- Forcing an apology without helping the child understand or repair.
