# Sportsmanship and teamwork

Keywords: game, games, lose, losing, lost, win, winning, won, team, teammate, teammates, sport, sports, soccer, compete, competition, play

## Key points
- Feeling disappointed after losing is normal; you do not have to pretend it feels good.
- Losing does not mean you are bad at something.
- Good sports calm down, congratulate the winner, and try again another time.
- Good teammates pass, cheer for others and stay kind when the team loses.
- Show these behaviors in action rather than explaining them.

## Avoid
- Making winning the reward for good behavior.
- Being mean to the winner or giving up forever.
