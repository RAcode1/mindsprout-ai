# Digital life and online safety

Keywords: screen, screens, screen time, tablet, phone, online, internet, game chat, personal information, address, password, stranger, social media, photos, images, privacy, share, sharing, group chat, viral, video, misinformation, true, fake, source

## Key points
- Screens are fun, and bodies and brains also need play, outdoor time and rest.
- It is okay to feel disappointed when screen time ends.
- Never share personal information like an address, school or password online.
- If anything online feels uncomfortable, tell a trusted adult; the child is not in trouble.
- Never share someone's private photos or messages without permission.
- Not everything online is true: check the source, look for evidence, and notice content designed to make you angry.

## Avoid
- Calling screens bad or scaring children about technology.
- Blaming a child who was targeted online.
- Explicit content in stories about online safety.
