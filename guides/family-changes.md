# Family changes

Keywords: new baby, sibling, brother, sister, divorce, separation, two homes, two houses, moving, move, parents, family

## Key points
- Reassure the child that they are loved just as much as before.
- Mixed feelings like jealousy, sadness or anger are normal during change.
- When parents separate, it is never the child's fault, and both parents can still love them.
- Keep routines steady where possible; predictability helps.
- Invite the child to talk about how they feel.

## Avoid
- Blaming either parent or asking the child to take sides.
- Promising that separated parents will get back together.
- Making a new baby or step-family member into a villain.
