# Responsibility

Keywords: chores, helping, help, responsibility, responsible, pet care, hamster, job, first job, reliable, on time, family, jobs

## Key points
- Everyone in a family helps; even small jobs matter.
- Helping should come from being part of the family, not only from rewards.
- Taking care of a pet means doing daily jobs because the pet depends on you.
- At a first job, reliability means showing up on time, speaking up when you can't, and owning mistakes.
- Show responsibility through what characters do, not lectures.

## Avoid
- Using a pet's death from neglect as a threat.
- Preachy explanations instead of story events.
