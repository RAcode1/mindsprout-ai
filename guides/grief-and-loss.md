# Grief and loss

Keywords: death, died, dies, dying, pet, loss, grief, grieving, goodbye, miss, missing, remember, funeral

## Key points
- Use honest, simple words: the pet died. Avoid phrases like "went to sleep" that confuse or frighten.
- Sadness is normal and can last a while. There is no deadline for grief.
- Remembering happy times, drawing, or telling stories about the one who died can help.
- Children may ask the same questions many times; answering patiently is part of grieving.
- Let the child see that grown-ups feel sad too.

## Avoid
- Rushing to replace a pet or telling a child to cheer up.
- Blaming the child for the loss.
- Graphic details about death.
- Bringing death or loss into a story when the parent didn't ask for help with a loss.
