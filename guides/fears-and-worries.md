# Fears and worries

Keywords: scared, afraid, fear, fears, dark, night, monsters, worry, worries, worried, anxious, nervous, new school, first day

## Key points
- Take fears seriously. To a child they feel real, even when the danger is not.
- Give practical comfort: a night light, a favorite toy, a grown-up nearby.
- Name the worry and talk about it; worries often shrink when shared.
- Slow breathing helps the body calm down.
- Small brave steps work better than forcing a child to face a big fear at once.

## Avoid
- Mocking or dismissing a fear ("that's silly", "there's nothing to worry about").
- Using scary characters or threats to make a child behave.
- Making a worry vanish by magic; show coping instead.
