# Friendship and disagreements

Keywords: friend, friends, friendship, disagree, disagreement, argument, argue, conflict, compromise, values, respect, drifting apart, change, group project

## Key points
- Friends sometimes disagree; that is normal.
- Saying how you feel and listening to the other person helps solve problems.
- Good solutions let both people feel heard; one person should not always give in.
- Friendships can change as people grow; drifting apart can be sad without anyone being bad.
- You can keep your values and still be kind and respectful.

## Avoid
- Making one friend the villain.
- Rewarding cruelty or turning a disagreement into a feud.
