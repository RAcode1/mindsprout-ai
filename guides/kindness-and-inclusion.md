# Kindness and inclusion

Keywords: kind, kindness, include, inclusion, alone, left out, lonely, gentle, hands, hit, hitting, differences, different, culture, cultures, religion, respect, welcome, new classmate

## Key points
- Everyone wants to feel included; inviting someone to join is a simple kindness.
- It is also okay for someone to say no to an invitation.
- Gentle hands with friends and pets; hitting hurts.
- Differences in food, language, holidays and traditions are worth being curious about.
- Every child in a story should be a full character, not a lesson prop.

## Avoid
- Portraying a lonely child as pitiful or a charity case.
- Stereotypes about any culture, religion or group.
- Mocking anyone's background or beliefs.
