# Healthy relationships

Keywords: dating, date, boyfriend, girlfriend, relationship, relationships, partner, crush, controlling, control, jealous, respect, trust, breakup, break up

## Key points
- Healthy relationships include respect, trust and space for your own friends and interests.
- Checking someone's phone or telling them who they can talk to are controlling behaviors and red flags.
- The person being controlled is never to blame.
- Breakups hurt; talking to friends or trusted adults helps.
- For young children, keep these topics to friendship and liking someone, with no romantic or physical content.

## Avoid
- Sexual or explicit content.
- Romance plots in stories for young children.
