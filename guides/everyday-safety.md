# Everyday safety

Keywords: safety, safe, danger, dangerous, risk, fire, matches, lighter, burn, driving, drive, car, texting, phone, seatbelt, license, crash

## Key points
- Matches and lighters are tools for grown-ups only; children should tell an adult if they find them.
- Drivers keep their eyes on the road: no texting or showing off, because no message is worth it.
- Seatbelts every time.
- Show safe choices and their good outcomes rather than graphic accidents.

## Avoid
- Making dangerous activities look fun.
- Graphic injuries, crashes or gore.
