# Health habits and routines

Keywords: bedtime, bed, sleep, sleepy, routine, bath, story, food, foods, eating, new food, taste, picky, teeth, brushing, brush, toothbrush

## Key points
- Predictable routines, like bath then story then bed, help children feel safe and sleepy.
- Trying a tiny bite of a new food is brave; children don't have to like it.
- It is okay to say "no thank you" after trying.
- Brushing teeth every day keeps them strong and healthy.
- For the youngest children, use very short sentences and lots of repetition.

## Avoid
- Pressure, shaming or "clean your plate" rules.
- Long or abstract text for toddlers.
