# Bullying and standing up for others

Keywords: bully, bullying, bullies, teasing, tease, mean, bystander, stand up, school bus, picked on, unkind

## Key points
- Children can help someone being teased by not joining in, staying with them, and being kind afterward.
- Telling a trusted adult is the right thing to do, not tattling.
- Nobody has to fight a bully to help.
- The person being teased is never to blame.

## Avoid
- Glorifying physical confrontation or revenge.
- Blaming the child who was bullied.
