# Body safety and boundaries

Keywords: body, touch, touching, private, consent, no, stop, hug, hugs, kiss, secret, secrets, gift, gifts, trusted adult, uncomfortable, safe, unsafe

## Key points
- Your body belongs to you. You can say no or stop to touch you don't like, even from someone you know.
- After saying no, tell a grown-up you trust.
- Safe secrets, like a surprise party, end soon. Unsafe secrets, especially ones a grown-up asks you to keep from your parents, should always be told.
- A grown-up who gives gifts and asks for secrecy from parents is a warning sign; tell a parent every time.
- Children are never in trouble for telling.

## Avoid
- Frightening stories with scary strangers.
- Shaming a relative or the child.
- Romantic or physical content in stories for young children.
